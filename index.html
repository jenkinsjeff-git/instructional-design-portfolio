Design approach

Start with the performance problem, not the authoring tool.

My process connects instructional decisions to the behavior, workflow, or operational outcome that needs to change. Technology is selected only after the learning challenge is clear.

  1. 1

    Define the business problem

    Identify the performance gap and the operational cost behind it.

  2. 2

    Design for realistic decisions and tasks

    Use practice, feedback, simulation, or branching when the work requires it.

  3. 3

    Select the right development tools

    Match Storyline, Rise, Camtasia, and supporting assets to the experience.

  4. 4

    Measure behavior beyond completion

    Connect learning evidence to observable workplace outcomes whenever possible.
